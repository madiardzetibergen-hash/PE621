Cat Titles & Pedigree Standards

In cat registries like TICA or CFA, cats earn official titles like Champion, Grand Champion, and Regional Winner based on show achievements.

Featured Show Champions

Champion (CH)

Awarded after winning a required number of official ribbons in show rings.

Grand Champion (GC)

Earned by defeating other Champions in open championship rings.

National Winner (NW)

The highest-scoring pedigree cats worldwide over a full show season.

Regional Winner (RW)

Awarded to top-tier cats competing within specific geographical regions.

Supreme Master

Highest title awarded to non-pedigree companion cats in show classes.

Junior Winner (JW)

Given to kittens and young cats demonstrating exceptional breed standards.
